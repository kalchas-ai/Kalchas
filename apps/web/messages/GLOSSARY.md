# Glossary

The product's own words, for whoever writes or translates the interface. Each is said one way everywhere in a language.
The tone is plain and calm: short sentences, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no jargon a newcomer would not know.

| English | What it is |
|---|---|
| world | What Kalchas builds out of the matter you bring: the real people, companies and institutions involved, and what they want |
| project | One matter and everything made for it (its world, its plays); "All projects" lists them |
| play | One run through a world, from the start to its end; "Back to this play" |
| stop | A point in a play where you decide what to do; the play moves from stop to stop |
| next step | The next stop ahead (never "gate" or "checkpoint") |
| timeline | One way a play has gone; going back to a decision and choosing again starts another timeline |
| decide / your move | What you do at a stop; "Your move" |
| hold | Choosing to do nothing for now |
| reactions | What everyone else does in answer to your move |
| leave it to chance / the odds | When something is settled by probability rather than by anyone's choice |
| the unexpected | Events no one chose (accidents, surprises) |
| people / person | Anyone in the world; a person's "profile" is who they are and what they want |
| side | A party in the matter (a person, a company, a group) |
| group | Many people taken together (customers, voters, a crowd) |
| outside force | Something in motion round the matter that no one in it controls (a market, a season, a rule coming in) |
| institution | A company, agency, court, university and the like, acting through its own process |
| key players | The people and institutions that matter most |
| you | The person playing; never "the protagonist" or "the user" in the interface |
| research | Live reading of the real world on the web while the world is built |
| findings / intel | What the research found |
| fact | One thing found to be true, with its source |
| source | Where a fact was read |
| build the world / building the world | Turning the findings into the world |
| understanding | Reading what you wrote before anything is asked |
| questions | What Kalchas asks you about the matter itself, one at a time |
| goal | What you want out of the matter |
| milestone | A point the matter may reach on the way |
| outcome | Where a play ended up, settled |
| review | Looking back over a play: the chain of causes, the lessons, the luck |
| compare (under the same conditions) | Setting two timelines side by side, with the outside world held the same |
| what really happened | For a matter in the past: the real history, to set beside your play |
| cost | What the model calls cost, in dollars |
| engine | The program that runs the world |
| Settings | The settings page |
